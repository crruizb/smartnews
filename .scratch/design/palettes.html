Smart News — palette candidates

Three editorial directions, each shown in light and dark and applied to the same pieces: wordmark, nav, source chips, a feature card, a compact card, star rating, and buttons. Headlines use Newsreader (serif display); UI uses Inter.

1 · Ink & Ember

warm · magazine · paper Recommended
Light
Dark

Warm cream paper and near-black ink with a burnt-orange accent. The most distinctly “printed magazine” of the three; reads premium and inviting, and the warm dark mode is unusually cozy for a news app.

2 · Ink & Cobalt

cool · classic · trustworthy
Light
Dark

Crisp paper-white with a deep cobalt accent on ink-navy text. Familiar, trustworthy, “established newspaper” energy (closest to NYT blue). Slightly cooler and more corporate than Ember.

3 · Ink & Plum

calm · modern · brand continuity
Light
Dark

Soft paper-white with a rich violet accent — a grown-up, high-contrast evolution of your current pastel purple. Keeps brand continuity while feeling far more intentional.
